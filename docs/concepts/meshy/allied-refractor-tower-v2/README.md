# Allied Refractor Tower V2

Allied refractive-energy defence. The approved silhouette has a broad four-foot base, tapered ceramic pylon, exposed vertical conduits, a sealed crown collar, one central faceted crystal, and two large flanking vanes.

## Contracts

- Preserve one central crystal and exactly two side vanes.
- Restore deterministic `Body` and `Head` hierarchy at the crown collar; rotation must remain sealed at 0, 45, 90, and 180 degrees.
- Front is the primary conduit/service face; origin is centered at ground level; runtime forward is `+Z`.
- Warm off-white ceramic is dominant. Cobalt follows boundary plates, graphite fills deep channels and the collar, satin metal follows conduits, and cyan/electric blue is reserved for the crystal and small energy windows.
- Avoid random color spots, a global blue wash, baked bloom, flat single-color treatment, soft geometry, or extra crown elements.
- Combined defence budget: 8,000–14,000 triangles, one shared PBR material, 1K base/normal and 512 packed metal-rough for shipping.
