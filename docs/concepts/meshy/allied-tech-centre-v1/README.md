# Allied Tech Centre v1

2x2 / 8 m Allied research building. A tall central laboratory drum is framed by two lower ceramic shoulders. It must read as a contained lab, not a power plant or radar station.

`geometry-sheet.png` and its four cardinal crops are the locked reconstruction source.

Material target: white ceramic shell, graphite vents, cobalt laboratory glass, chrome coil rings, cyan core accents, restrained amber entry lights.
