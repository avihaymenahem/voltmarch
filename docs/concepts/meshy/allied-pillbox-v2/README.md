# Allied Pillbox V2

Compact fixed defence for the Allied roster. The approved geometry is a low ceramic casemate with a deep protected embrasure, one barrel, readable roof/service panels, and no turret articulation.

## Contracts

- Preserve exactly one barrel and the deep dark embrasure.
- Front is the firing face; origin is centered at ground level; runtime forward is `+Z`.
- Warm off-white ceramic is the dominant material. Blue is restricted to coherent armor-boundary panels, graphite to recesses and vents, cyan to optics, and metal to the barrel and hardware.
- Avoid random faction-color spots, global blue/white washes, soft blob geometry, baked lighting, and mirror-smooth paint.
- Static defence budget: 8,000–14,000 triangles, one shared PBR material, 1K base/normal and 512 packed metal-rough for shipping.

`geometry-sheet.png` is the cardinal geometry contract. `base-color.png` is the approved material reference. The procedural version remains a load-failure fallback only after integration.
