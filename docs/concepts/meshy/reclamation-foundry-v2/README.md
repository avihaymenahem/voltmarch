# Reclamation Foundry v2 — reconstruction-safe geometry contract

V1 was rejected before texture because Meshy mirrored its tall fabrication mast into two towers. V2 removes
all freestanding towers and carries the faction asymmetry in the fused building body.

## Runtime target

- Heavy low 3x3 construction headquarters with a clear front deployment bay and ramp.
- Exactly one short, thick fabrication arm integrated into the physical left shoulder.
- No crane, tower, antenna, cable, truss, floating part, or mirrored arm.
- One offset two-ring arc coil; broad charcoal, warm-metal, violet and amber material regions.
- Geometry budget: 22k-35k triangles at LOD0 after local retopology.
- Procedural Foundry remains the load-failure fallback until the imported replacement passes every gate.
