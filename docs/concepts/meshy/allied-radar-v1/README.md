# Allied Radar Dome v1

2x2 / 12 m Allied sensor building. The locked silhouette is a low ceramic base, central mast, and one dominant directional dish. The dish is a visual scanner, not a second command-post aerial. Keep the doorway and broad vent fields readable at RTS distance.

`geometry-sheet.png` is the approved neutral-clay four-view source. `front.png`, `right.png`, `back.png`, and `left.png` are the exact Meshy inputs.

Material target: white ceramic armour, graphite mechanical recesses, chrome joints, cobalt glass, cyan status light, restrained amber service light. No flat all-white pass.
