# Allied Repair Depot v1

2x2 / 6.5 m Allied vehicle service structure. The front approach and central service deck must remain open. Two curved pylons support one solid overhead beam and compact hoist; no lattice crane or dangling cable geometry.

`geometry-sheet.png` and its four cardinal crops are the locked reconstruction source.

Material target: white ceramic pylons, graphite tread deck and machinery, chrome beam/hoist, cobalt inspection glass, cyan work indicators, amber downlights.
