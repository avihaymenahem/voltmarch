# Civilian Oil Derrick v1

Neutral capturable 2x2 / 8 m x 8 m oil site, approximately 13 m tall. Its silhouette is defined by a single heavy pumpjack, compact pump house, cylindrical separator tank and thick exposed transfer pipes. The frame deliberately uses broad box girders instead of fine lattice so it survives RTS camera distance and later LOD reduction.

`geometry-sheet.png` and its four cardinal crops are the locked reconstruction source.

Material target: sun-faded galvanized steel, desaturated industrial yellow on the walking beam, dark graphite machinery, oxidized pipe joints, dusty concrete and restrained oil staining. It must remain faction-neutral and visibly distinct from Allied architecture.
