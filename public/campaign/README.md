# Campaign portraits

The nineteen portraits in `portraits/` are original AI-assisted character artwork
generated for VOLTMARCH with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on
2026-08-21. They cover the recurring cast: Rakhalt, Vosk, Wend, Aubray,
Calvane, Nael, Tallow, Cregg, Bramm, Hesk, Oreth, Ardle, Averill, Merrow,
Rathe, Sennet, Bardin, Skell and Tolvar. They are
campaign/interface assets, not models
or textures used by the procedural game world.

The portraits were authored as one visual set: fictional identities, matching
command-compartment lighting and square framing that survives both the briefing
card and the smaller in-match communications crop. The March uses charcoal and
red, the Allies navy and cyan, the Pact black and violet, and Reclamation worn
olive, brass and amber. The source generations remain in the local Codex
generated image store; the shipped WebP files are 640 x 640 delivery derivatives.
